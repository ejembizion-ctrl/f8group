About Fortun8 Group

Founded on Vision, Integrity & Excellence

Fortun8 Group is a multi-sector parent company established to incubate, scale, and direct high-impact enterprises across real estate, education, community development, and digital technology.

Leadership

Boniface Ejembi

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Boniface Ejembi is a distinguished leader, sales manager, and strategist with extensive multi-disciplinary expertise. A graduate in Chemical Engineering from the Federal University of Technology, Minna, he also pursues advanced studies in Information Technology with a specialization in Cybersecurity at Miva Open University.

With over six years of professional sales and administrative management experience, Boniface brings rigorous academic grounding, sharp business acumen, and an unwavering commitment to societal transformation through Fortun8 Group.
